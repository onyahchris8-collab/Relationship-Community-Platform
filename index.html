The foundation

Built for the single, the married — and the girl-child.

Florence Singles and Married Global Network Foundation is a relationship-education and advocacy platform. We teach the unglamorous parts of love: emotional attention, honest boundaries, repair after conflict, and the courage to ask for what you actually need.

Through online forums, coaching conversations and social media, The Love Doctor meets people where they are — dating, engaged, years into marriage, or starting over — and gives them language for the work love requires.

“Most people don’t need a perfect partner. They need language for what they are feeling — and permission to ask for it.”
